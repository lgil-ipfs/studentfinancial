Who We Are

Money stress is one of the biggest barriers to student success — and we're here to remove it.

StudentFinancial.ca is a an educational resource created by Iberian Pacific Financial Services Inc. to make financial literacy accessible and judgment-free for every Canadian student. We offer one-on-one sessions with Iberian Pacific advisors, plain-English guides, and tools built specifically for the Canadian system. There's no cost to use these resources, and no obligation to become an Iberian Pacific client.

Our mission

To give every Canadian student the financial knowledge and support they need to thrive — regardless of their background or income.
