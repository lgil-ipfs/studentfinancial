How this calculator works

  • 0% Federal Interest: Canada Student Loans currently accrue no interest.
  • Provincial Variations: Different provinces have different rates — BC and several others are 0%, while Ontario is Prime + 1%, Quebec is Prime + 0.5%, and Alberta, Saskatchewan, and NT follow Prime.
  • Live Prime Rate: The Bank of Canada overnight rate plus a 2.20% spread is fetched automatically from the BoC public API.
  • Grace Period: The 6-month post-graduation grace period is modelled, including interest accrual on any loans with variable rates.
  • RAP: The Repayment Assistance Plan is modelled in two stages — Stage 1 (months 1–60) where the government covers remaining interest, and Stage 2 (60+ months) where the government contributes to principal as well.

Results are estimates based on inputs provided. This tool does not constitute financial advice. RAP eligibility and payment amounts are determined by the National Student Loans Service Centre (NSLSC). Always confirm your loan details with your lender.

Enable Repayment Assistance (RAP)?

To qualify for the federal Repayment Assistance Plan, you must meet the following criteria:

  • Loans must be in repayment — at least 6 months post-graduation
  • Loans must be in good standing (not in default)
  • Monthly income must fall below qualifying thresholds
  • You must reapply every 6 months
  • Note: RAP applies to federal Canada Student Loans only — provincial assistance is calculated separately by each province.
